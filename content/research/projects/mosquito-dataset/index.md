## Overview

This work provides an open global evidence base for studying mosquito
invasions. It brings together non-native distribution records for more than
180 disease-vector mosquito species with biological, ecological, and
macroecological traits, source documentation, reproducible code, and an
interactive visualisation.

## My contribution

I compiled records from scientific literature, databases, and other sources
and harmonised, quality-checked, and documented the data. I developed a consistent structure linking distribution records with
species traits, created reproducible workflows to determine environmental thresholds for their ecological niche, as well as to process and analyse the data. I contributed to the conceptualisation of the visualisation tool and the scientific manuscript. I coordinated the project across four institutions: NOVA University Lisbon’s Institute of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine, the University of Lisbon’s Institute of Geography and Spatial Planning, Heidelberg University’s Interdisciplinary Center for Scientific Computing, and the Colorado School of Public Health’s Center for Global Health.
