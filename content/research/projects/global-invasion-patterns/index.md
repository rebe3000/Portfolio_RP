## Overview

In this project we reconstruct where, when, and how disease-vector mosquitoes have
spread through human activity. We combine global introduction records with
spatial, temporal, socioeconomic, and environmental information to reveal
historical trends, geographic patterns (e.g., hot and cold spots of introductions), and drivers of mosquito invasions.

## My contribution

I contributed to compiling, checking, and harmonising the global invasion
records used in the study. I developed reproducible workflows in R, conducted
spatial and temporal analyses, investigated global patterns and drivers,
produced publication-quality maps and visualisations, and contributed to
interpreting the results and writing the manuscript. I coordinated this work between researchers from NOVA University Lisbon’s Institute of Hygiene and Tropical Medicine, the University of Lisbon’s Institute of Geography and Spatial Planning, and University Vienna's Department of Botany and Biodiversity Research
