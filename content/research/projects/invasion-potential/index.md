## Overview

In this project we used biological, ecological, and macroecological traits to assess
which disease-vector mosquitoes have the potential to spread beyond their
native ranges, but have not yet done so. Using random forest models we estimate probabilities of introduction
and establishment, providing evidence for proactive surveillance and species
prioritisation.

## My contribution

I contributed to developing the research question and analytical workflow,
prepared and integrated species-trait and invasion data, and implemented the
predictive analyses in R. This included addressing incomplete trait information, testing various machine learning methods, comparing and validating models,
interpreting species-level predictions, producing visualisations, and
contributing to the manuscript.
