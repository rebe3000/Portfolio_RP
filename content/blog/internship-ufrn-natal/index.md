During a research internship at the Federal University of Rio Grande do Norte
(UFRN) in Brazil, I investigated fossil assemblages from the Santana Formation.
I processed rock samples to isolate microfossils, documented specimens using
scanning electron microscopy (SEM), and identified ostracod species based on
their morphological characteristics.

The resulting data contributed to reconstructing the formation’s past
environmental and climatic conditions.
