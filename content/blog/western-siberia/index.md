The excursion took us through Western Siberia from Tyumen (Тюмень) to
Khanty-Mansiysk (Ханты-Мансийск). Along the route, we examined how oil
production, infrastructure development, and urbanisation are transforming
landscapes, as well as the transition from extensive to intensive agriculture.

Field visits introduced us to the region’s floodplains and peatlands and to the
role of fire regimes in shaping these ecosystems. The excursion connected
landscape-ecological observations with discussions of the social and economic
drivers of environmental change.

The programme was also an opportunity for cultural exchange with Russian
students. We stayed in student accommodation and experienced everyday local
life—including a traditional banya.
