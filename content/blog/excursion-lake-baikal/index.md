The summer school at Lake Baikal consisted of various day trips focusing on
plants and landscape types. We visited the driest areas of the Tasheran Steppe
as well as extremely wet, marshy sites. Early in the morning, there were also
birdwatching excursions, and in the afternoon we had an hour of Russian
language lessons. We also went on trips to Olkhon Island, to a cave, and to a
banya.

This summer school was part of the DAAD-funded Go East summer school programme,
which aims to promote cultural exchange between German students and partners
from Central, South-Eastern and Eastern Europe, as well as the South Caucasus
and Central Asia.
