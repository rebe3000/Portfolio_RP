What happens after lignite opencast mining? This excursion examined
biochemical processes in lakes with varying degrees of acidification following
opencast lignite mining in an area now used as a local recreation area.

We visited active and recultivated mining landscapes and applied field methods
to study water chemistry and biogeochemical processes in post-mining lakes.
